### Highlights

- Settings › Cloud has a new Models page: turn a model on, set the provider key, pick a machine and its hours, and watch its state.
- Manage people, keys and usage from the Models page — block a single key or a whole person, and see when each was last used.
- Sign in to the cloud with a magic link that brings you straight back into the app.
- Consultations: enrol a Hub, follow the central system's health, and read the last 30 days of consultations.
- The top bar now shows your cloud account, and Settings › Cloud reads as a set of titled account cards.

### Features

- New Models page in Settings › Cloud, with its own section in the Cloud picker and in Settings search.
- Set a model up end to end: activation, provider key, machine and running hours.
- The model machine shows its state, its commands and a refresh action, with the GPU named as the catalog names it.
- The model form reads the provider catalog, names the candidate that is running and explains refusals.
- Owners see every person, key, block and usage figure on the Models page, and can block a single key.
- Members see their own keys, their creation and last-use dates, and the cloud's API address in Settings › Cloud › Models.
- New Settings › Cloud › Debra page with the support address, reachable from Settings search.
- «Reset my Debra identity» signs the Hub out of the cloud.
- The Debra settings tell you to update Chaxter when the cloud is running a different mode.
- Desktop builds can be given a Debra origin so the page opens from the app.
- The cloud console is an opt-in section of Settings, in Chaxter's look, listing your organisation's accounts.
- Cloud audit entries and every cloud timestamp are shown in your language.
- Mail settings explain a refused sender, a refused envelope and a refused save, and the cloud's mail account is set from the Cloud section.
- Settings › Cloud is grouped into titled blocks, with a once-shown value presented as its own card.
- The top bar shows the cloud account, and Cloud reads as an account card.
- On a cloud's first start, the app claims the owner account for you.
- Read-only Consultations page showing your consultations of the last 30 days.
- Enrol a consult Hub, read the central system's health, and enrol again with a new code if the Hub was removed.
- Fill in a repository's consultation settings and mark the repository that answers a project's consultations.
- Consultations run each agent in an isolated sandbox, with test runs capped and recorded, their own seat budget, and a full audit kept for 30 days.

### Bug Fixes

- Settings fits a 320 px window, scrolls properly, and collapses its header into one row in short windows.
- A reply that lands while you are typing no longer steals your field, your text or your focus.
- Fields reached by keyboard are brought fully into view, including under the pinned heading and at 200 % zoom.
- Settings scrolling follows your hand: a reveal stops a glide in flight, and movement you make is never overridden.
- The Cloud page picker keeps a stable layout, falling back from two rows to one column when the width does not allow it.
- Settings search finds Cloud › Mail by email, e-mail and posta, and opens the page on what it found.
- Escape and confirmed actions hand the keyboard back to the control you pressed, and a newly issued ticket lands on its Copy button.
- The Cloud Integrations, Audit and Hubs tables stay inside their own boxes and scroll there; long Hub names wrap instead of stretching the layout.
- The mail form names a refused setting at its own field, in your language, and keeps what you typed.
- The cloud account badge and the top bar fold cleanly on narrow and phone widths, and an account email breaks at its «@».
- The sign-in link page is styled as Chaxter Cloud, explains what happens before it asks, and its Italian wording matches the English.
- A superseded or refused sign-in is explained again on the form, and clears once the address changes.
- The Cloud section shows the session the Hub holds, whichever window signed in.
- The baseline-lost banner says why it was lost and what Modified files compares now.
- Pinned tabs open a consultation read-only from its address, and give up waiting after 15 seconds instead of hanging.
- Consultation views read better: the question wraps, the hover card names its state, dead links no longer show a raw id, and panels that do not apply say so.
- Consultations end cleanly when a team member or its lead dies, when they are cancelled, or when the Hub is killed, and leftover agent folders are swept at boot.
- «Copied» now refers to the value on screen and tells you when a copy failed.
- A terminal opens without waiting for the agent to spawn, using a key issued per window.
- A session start the agent never answers is refused after 90 seconds instead of hanging.
- A tab held behind another window's reload keeps its conversation.
- Transcript rows accept a press while the list is still scrolling.
- Composer bar rows wrap at 400 px, and the reset button sits under its title.
- The limits fields accept only what the Hub actually applies, with shorter captions and the detail moved to their hint.
- On Windows, a shell that starts too slowly is reported instead of failing silently, and a slow start no longer breaks a sample.
- A server switched off stays off on OpenCode 2.

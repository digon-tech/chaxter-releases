### Highlights

- Tool calls render as a single card with states — it never moves, never collapses on its own, and long output has a height limit.
- Chaxter scans this machine for conversations your installed agents already hold and lets you adopt one, history intact.
- Every session row carries a state mark with its own colour and icon, explained in Settings, plus a hover card with the full title and state in words.
- The sidebar can be dense or relaxed, and the session list can be narrowed to the current workspace.
- Build a team from a list of trades, write a role yourself, and change how one member works from its own pane.

### Features

- Tool calls render as one card with states: no change of place, no automatic collapse, and a ceiling on very long output.
- Reasoning rows show what the agent thought and mark the one still running, with their own height limit.
- Cards open and close on a real spring, and a card that has been shown is never taken away again.
- The transcript holds your reading position by hand, so streaming output no longer drags the view around.
- Jumping to a message opens a page around it and fills in as you scroll.
- A reopened conversation paints the last few screens immediately and loads the rest on request.
- Chaxter scans this machine for conversations held by your installed agents and lists them alongside your own.
- Adopt a found conversation to open it with its history in place.
- Six session states, each with its own colour, icon and animation, with a legend in Settings explaining every mark.
- Session rows carry a hover card with the whole title and the state in words, and you can keep its full form open by preference.
- Dense and relaxed sidebar rows, with a density switch in the header and a per-browser preference.
- Narrow the session list to a single workspace from the menu it already has.
- The sidebar rail is drawn as a graph, so an open and focused row is legible without relying on colour.
- Remove an agent you added yourself, with a confirmation.
- Build a team from a list of trades, or write a role yourself and save it for every tab to reuse.
- A team you asked for arrives already written in the message box, unsent, with its way of working chosen.
- Rewrite how one team member must work, and read back the instructions it is under, from its own pane.
- A lead can let a finished member go and keep its record, or hand a decision back to a person.
- A team's row says in what way the team works, and the creation window names the engine that leads.
- A silent round is reported, and the person can close it.

### Bug Fixes

- A link into a settings section lands where you asked and holds its place as the column grows.
- The settings rail folds to its icons in a narrow window, and its sections are reachable with the keyboard.
- A turn you watched keeps every tool row it drew, and a turn drawn folded stays folded for its life.
- A pane draws no rows until its replay has all of them, so history stops arriving in pieces.
- Reopening a conversation lands in one jump instead of scrolling you through the whole history.
- The reasoning shown is the reasoning the agent sent, and only the live row carries the running mark.
- Live replies render their markdown as they stream: lists, links and emphasis heal instead of flashing raw syntax.
- A table wraps its last column instead of widening past the pane, and a line break inside a cell is a line break.
- The session list's first paint stops mounting every row, and one update now re-renders one card.
- Two workspace folders of the same name are told apart, and one directory is one section however its path is spelled.
- Folder sections keep their place and their order while you are reading the list.
- One press of «show all» reads the uncapped folder once, and a saved «show all» is honoured on the next visit.
- Search finds a session by the handle its row prints, and every cut title carries its ellipsis.
- A session row's hover card fits the screen, never covers its own row, and closes on a click until the pointer comes back.
- Selection and hover cover the whole session row, and the selected outline no longer nudges the layout.
- A team member's pane reads as read-only on every connection and names the team's session to write to.
- A member's open question and permission ask reach every tab and are answered on the team's session.
- A team's cost total keeps its members' share across a restart and never drops when a member ends or is revived.
- Stopping a team reads as cancelled on every member it cuts, and a stopped member says so on its row.
- A member that was let go, uninstalled or could not be revived says so on its row and hover card, with how to bring it back.
- Permission dialogs keep the scope control in view, break long paths at a slash, and stack their options at full width.
- The new-session dialog fits the screen, keeps its position, marks an invalid folder as you type, and accepts a typed path as a place to open.
- One message for a server outage instead of three, with retry held until the connection is back.
- A workspace panel closed by an outage comes back with the connection, and a send the server is holding is no longer reported as failed.
- Archived and trashed sessions read as read-only, and the empty-trash dialog counts every session and keeps its numbers while it runs.
- Palette rows that read alike carry their last activity to the second, and an insert no longer lands in a field the session has taken away.
- A custom agent with nothing to install says so, an absolute command path runs the file it names, and a program missing on this host is refused with a reason.
- Italian wording repaired throughout: accents, stray commas, and words that should never have been translated.
- Sending with the pointer hands the keyboard back to the message box, and Escape closes one layer at a time.
- A session that moved to a new id opens on the conversation it moved to, and a pane lets go of a session closed underneath it.
- A session's saved title follows the agent's latest name instead of freezing on the first one.
- A prompt the server is still holding is no longer drawn as sent, and the sidebar stops saying «Working» while it waits.

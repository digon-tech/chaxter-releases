### Highlights

- A new MCP bridge lets external tools connect to the app; it stays off until you turn it on in Preferences and unlock it with a one-time passcode.
- A dock panel shows the bridge's live calls, its port and connected clients, with a control to close the connection.
- A top-bar badge lights up each time the bridge handles a call.
- The desktop app now watches its background service and starts it again if it disappears, backing off between attempts until it stays up.

### Features

- Turn the MCP bridge on or off in Preferences, with a one-time passcode and a lockout after repeated failures.
- A bridge panel in the dock shows calls as they arrive and lets you close the connection.
- A top-bar badge lights up on every bridge call.
- The bridge is off by default in release builds and only opens behind a one-time passcode.
- The desktop app watches its background service and restarts it when it goes away.

### Bug Fixes

- A failed copy of the bridge passcode now says so right under the passcode.
- The top bar re-lays out immediately when the bridge badge appears, and the badge folds away with the rest of the bar, last and to its right.
- The bridge port is shown beside the passcode and in the panel header.
- A call the bridge does not recognise is drawn grey and marked as not supported.
- The bridge panel counts connections rather than agents.
- The bridge panel no longer says the connection is closed once an agent is back.
- The bridge panel keeps its close control visible while the call list scrolls.
- The bridge no longer errors out when it cannot listen to the app shell.
- The version shown in the app now reports the installed release.
- The retry schedule for restarting the background service only resets after it has stayed up for a minute, and a first start that failed is retried on the same schedule.
- A recovery notice now dismisses only the restart message it raised.
- An authenticated request keeps the desktop background service awake.
